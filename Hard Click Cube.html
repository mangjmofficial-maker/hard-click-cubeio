Drag to spin the cube any direction, even upside down. R L U D F B always follow your view, so turn the cube around and L becomes R. Keys: letter = turn, Shift = prime.

R L U D F B – turn the Right, Left, Up, Down, Front, Back face 90° clockwise as you look at that face.

X' – prime: counter-clockwise. X2 – a half turn (180°).

M E S – middle slices. M follows L, E follows D, S follows F.

r l u d f b – wide turns: the face plus the middle slice with it.

x y z – rotate the whole cube like R, U, F.

OLL (Orient Last Layer) makes the whole top face one color. PLL (Permute Last Layer) then moves the top pieces into their right places. Together with the cross and F2L they finish the CFOP method.

How to practise: press Setup to jump the cube into the case, then Play to watch the algorithm solve it step by step.
